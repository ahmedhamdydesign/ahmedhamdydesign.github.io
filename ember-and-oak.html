Our Story

Cooked slowly.
Served warmly.

We started with one oak-fired grill and a simple belief: food tastes better when it takes its time. Today our kitchen works entirely over live fire — no shortcuts, no freezers, just farmers we know by name and a menu that changes with the seasons.
